Frequently Asked Questions

What is Tocky?

Tocky is a simple, playful timer that helps children understand how much time remains for an activity.

Why didn’t the timer alert sound?

  • Make sure your device volume is turned up.
  • Check that Silent Mode or Focus is not preventing alerts.
  • Keep notifications enabled for Tocky in Settings.

Does Tocky require an Internet connection?

No. Tocky’s timer works offline.

Does Tocky collect personal data?

No. Tocky does not collect or transmit personal data.
