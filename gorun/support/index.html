Frequently Asked Questions

How do I transfer music to Apple Watch?

  1. Open GoRun on your iPhone.
  2. Select the songs you want to transfer.
  3. Keep your iPhone and Apple Watch nearby until the transfer completes.

Why can’t I find my music on Apple Watch?

  • Make sure Bluetooth and Wi-Fi are enabled.
  • Ensure the transfer has completed successfully.
  • Restart GoRun on Apple Watch if necessary.

Does GoRun require an Internet connection?

No. Once your music has been imported, playback works offline.

Does GoRun collect my personal data?

No. GoRun does not collect or transmit your personal data.
