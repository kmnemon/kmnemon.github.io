Offline music for Apple Watch

Take Your Music Anywhere

Transfer your local music to Apple Watch and enjoy offline playback without carrying your iPhone.

Designed for Apple Watch

GoRun is built around Apple Watch.

Your iPhone is used to organize and transfer music, while Apple Watch becomes your portable offline music player.

Whether you’re running, working out, traveling, or simply leaving your phone behind, GoRun lets you enjoy your favorite music directly from your Apple Watch.

Features

Apple Watch

  • Play music offline without your iPhone
  • Browse songs, artists, albums, and playlists
  • Compatible with AirPods media controls
  • Support repeat and shuffle playback
  • Adjust volume using the Digital Crown

iPhone

  • Transfer local music to Apple Watch wirelessly
  • Import music from the Files app
  • Transfer music over Wi-Fi
  • Browse and organize your music library
  • Create and manage playlists for Apple Watch and iPhone

Perfect For

  • Gym workouts
  • Walking
